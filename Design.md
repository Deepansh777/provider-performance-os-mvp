# VBP SAAS PRODUCT STRATEGY

## Goal: Build a sellable, sticky, executive-friendly SaaS platform for ACOs, Provider Groups, PCP Organizations, Brokers, and Value-Based Care Operators

---

# PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

## Core Rule:

Users do not pay for “8 tabs of analytics.”

## Users pay for:

### “Show me where I’m losing money, why, and what to do next.”

---

# PRODUCT NORTH STAR:

## Measure → Benchmark → Dollarize → Prioritize → Operationalize

---

# RECOMMENDED 5-TAB PRODUCT STRUCTURE

---

# TAB 1 — PERFORMANCE COMMAND CENTER

## Purpose:

Executive summary of provider/group VBP performance across financial, quality, access, hospital, and referral domains.

## Primary Buyer:

- CEO
- CFO
- ACO President
- Broker
- Practice Owner

---

## Business Questions Answered:

- How much VBP incentive is available?
- How much did we earn?
- Where are we underperforming?
- Which domain is costing us the most?

---

## VISUALS

### VISUAL 1 — KPI HEADER CARDS

**Visual Type:** KPI Tiles

**Metrics:**

- Total Available Pool — R12 ($)

- Missed Opportunity — R12 ($)

- Attributed Members (Current Month)
- Avg Risk Score (R12)

**Visual Type:** Heatmap / Domain Scorecard Table

**Domains:**

- Access & Timeliness

**Fields:**

- Weight
- R12 Score
- Benchmark
- vs Benchmark
- Available ($)
- Earned ($)
- Missed ($)
- Capture %

### VISUAL 3 — EARNINGS WATERFALL

**Visual Type:** Waterfall Chart

**Metrics:**

- Access Earned ($)
